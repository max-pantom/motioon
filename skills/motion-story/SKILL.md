# Story

Do not open `motion.md` until you can say the tape in one line.

## Spine

Write these four lines, nothing else:

- Audience — who is watching, in one noun phrase
- Situation — what they already believe
- Turn — what changes in the tape
- Promise — what they know at  the last hold

If the turn is “it has features,” you do not have a story. Name one change.

## Forms (pick one)

- Punch — 6–10s. One turn. Card / object / card.
- Launch — 8–15s. Card, product tape, name, hold.
- Explain — 12–25s. Problem line, one demo, one result. No tour.
- Quote — 6–8s. One sentence, designed breaks, almost no move.

For a launch clip, choose one product action instead of a long feature tour.
If the user asks for a walkthrough, show the sequence that explains the task.

## Copy rules

- One idea per scene. The line is the scene.
- No slogan stacking. If a line can go, it goes.
- Voice is the product talking, not a narrator announcing features.
- Last line is a name or a still. Not a URL unless asked.

## Handoff

Pass the spine + form name into `motion-storyboard`. Do not invent shots first.
