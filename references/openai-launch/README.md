# Six local OpenAI product films

These six sheets describe the exact user-provided files in
`/Users/macbook/Downloads/openai-train-vibe`. They are evidence for the
`openai-launch` recipe, not reusable video assets. Durations and frame rates
come from FFmpeg metadata. `detected_cuts_*` are threshold detections, not
ground-truth edit decisions; low-contrast white UI transitions are easy to
miss. Shot boundaries and copy are visual estimates from 2-second contact
sheets. Audio is present in all six, but exact sound onsets were not measured.

Observed pattern: four films clearly use sparse cards around captured product
flows; one is almost entirely UI; one has only brief title treatment before
the UI. The product flow is the shared element. White and ink dominate cards,
while product footage can bring its own blue, teal, violet, or multicolor
assets. Hard cuts and long UI holds matter more than enforcing a single palette
across the entire video.

The sixth film, Word, changes the recipe: its blue framed product tape proves
that a UI shot may use one contextual product backdrop. The Drive film changes
the sequence rule: cards are optional if the captured flow explains itself.
